About

I am a mathematician at the Johannes Kepler University Linz, working at the interface of numerical analysis, information-based complexity, and high-dimensional probability.

I am a Senior Scientist at both the Institute of Analysis and the Department of Quantum Computing at JKU. I completed my Habilitation in Linz in 2023, after a PhD in Jena under Erich Novak.

My work received the 2021 Joseph F. Traub Prize for Achievement in Information-Based Complexity, awarded for contributions to the numerics and complexity of high-dimensional integration and approximation, and in particular the power of random information.

In 2026 I was invited to write a survey for Acta Numerica (vol. 35), the leading review journal in numerical analysis, which I co-authored with David Krieg. “Approximation of functions: optimal sampling and complexity” draws together a decade of work on sampling recovery and the gap between optimal and random information.
